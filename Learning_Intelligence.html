Orbit AI Learning

Orbit AI is the intelligence inside PLS. It learns from how your team works and what gets results, then tunes PLS to fit your team. Every learning applies automatically. This page shows what Orbit AI learned, why, and the data behind it. Visible to the Owner and admins only.

How Orbit Is Learning
Over the last 14 weeks, Orbit has watched 1,846 of your team’s calls and 9 campaigns to see which owners pick up, which mailers get replies, and which leads turn into listings. It used that to put the most promising owners at the top of your call list and to call each type of owner at the time they answer most. The result: your team reaches 22% more owners for the same number of calls, which means more conversations, more appointments and more listings.
