## Orbit AI Mobile (added 2026-11-15)

`mobile/` is the iPhone companion mockup for Orbit AI PRD v0.5 §15: find an owner or property, touch history, Attack Brief, ask the brief, and voice debrief with the bundled Save all card. Open `mobile/` on a laptop for the phone frame and the review guide, or on an iPhone in Safari (Share › Add to Home Screen) for full screen. Voice is scripted; nothing is stored. Every desktop page's footer links to it.
